This website only processes the data it needs to be served and to count visits. It has no forms or accounts, sets no cookies, doesn't profile you and doesn't sell your data. This page explains what is processed, why, who processes it, for how long, and how to exercise your rights. {% .lead %}

## What data is processed

- **Serving the website:** to deliver a page, Vercel's servers necessarily receive your IP address and the technical details of your request, such as the page asked for and your browser's user agent.
- **Visit statistics:** to understand which pages are read and how visitors find them, each page view records the date and time, the page address, the referring website, campaign parameters in the address such as `utm_source`, the approximate location (country, region and city), the operating system, the browser and their versions, and the device type. This is done with [Vercel Web Analytics](https://vercel.com/docs/analytics), as explained below.
- **Theme preference:** if you use the theme switcher, your choice of light, dark or system is saved in your browser's local storage. It stays on your device, and it isn't used to identify you or link your visits together. See the [cookie notice](/cookies).
- **Email:** if you write to me, I process your email address, any name you give and the content of your message, to read and answer it.

This website has no contact form, no accounts, no comments and no error tracking.

---

## How IP addresses are handled

To count visits without cookies, Vercel Web Analytics groups the page views of the same visitor using a hash calculated from the request, which resets every 24 hours. Visits can't be linked across different days or different websites, and the statistics it stores don't include your IP address. I only see them as aggregated figures.

## Legal bases for processing

- **Serving the website and visit statistics:** the legitimate interest in delivering this website, keeping it secure, and understanding how it is used, under Article 6(1)(f) of the [GDPR](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:02016R0679-20160504). The data collected is limited to what these purposes need.
- **Email:** the legitimate interest in answering your message, under Article 6(1)(f) of the GDPR.
- **Theme preference:** it is only stored when you choose it, and only to remember that choice. Under these conditions it is exempt from consent under Article 22.2 of [Spanish Law 34/2002 (LSSI-CE)](https://www.boe.es/buscar/doc.php?id=BOE-A-2002-13758), as part of a service you explicitly request.

None of the processing described here relies on consent. If any future processing requires it, I'll ask for it first, and you'll be able to withdraw it at any time.

## How long data is kept

Visit statistics are kept for the periods set by Vercel for Web Analytics. Emails are kept for as long as needed to handle the conversation, then deleted, unless a legal obligation requires keeping them. Your theme preference stays on your device until you clear this website's data from your browser.

## Your rights

The GDPR gives you the right to ask what data about you is processed and receive a copy, to have it corrected or deleted, and, where the law provides, to object to its processing, restrict it, or take it with you under Article 20.

To exercise these rights, email [privacy@lucapipolo.com](mailto:privacy@lucapipolo.com). You'll receive a response within one month, subject to the extensions Article 12 of the GDPR allows. Where data is genuinely anonymous, such as aggregated visit statistics, it isn't possible to find the data of a particular person.

If you believe your data hasn't been handled lawfully, you can complain to Spain's data protection authority, the [Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (AEPD)](https://www.aepd.es), or to the supervisory authority where you live, work, or where the alleged infringement took place.
